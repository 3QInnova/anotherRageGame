# Fake-flag validation

`node tests/fake-flags.test.mjs` checks that walking through the ground decoy is safe; jumping into the elevated field, including the habitual jump over the original floor flag, causes a real rejection death; and retries retain the actual learned identity. It also checks rightward, leftward, and stationary drop capture; visible horizontal motion during the warning; target stability after a direction change; safe passage through the old claim; death at the revised impact; bait/wait/cross counterplay; and skipping passed drops after reset. All twenty new authored remarks are unique within these pools and rotate before repeating.

`node tests/long-level.test.mjs` completes the revised long level with zero deaths and one or two actual compactions across five seeds. All ten compaction return points have ordinary-input zero-death recovery routes. The normal seven-room campaign route is regenerated by `tests/forecast-feedback.test.mjs`. Existing loss reading, observer, autocomplete, floor-deception, and earlier-room compaction checks pass.

Headless Chromium in `tests/fake-flags-browser.test.mjs` checks safe floor claims, actual airborne rejection death and two-press retry, captured target motion, revised active impact, bait/wait recovery, narrow-screen overflow, and no page errors. These isolated fixtures disable unrelated hazards to inspect outcome boundaries. The separate full campaign replay uses ordinary keyboard inputs with active hazards and passes all seven rooms with zero deaths, retaining forecast cues, loss reading/skip, and pointer/touch checks. The revised long-level browser practice replay also passes with actual compaction and zero deaths.

[Runtime results](runtime-results.json), [browser results](browser-results.json), and [new remarks](remark-library.json) record the dedicated evidence. Long-level seed and recovery results remain in [long-level validation](../long-level/VALIDATION.md).

## Limits

The five-seed completion check is sampled rather than exhaustive. Fixtures verify collision and warning boundaries, while the full route verifies reachability; neither establishes player enjoyment, broad browser compatibility, or native-mobile usability. Observe whether first-time players distinguish a stale claim from the newly revealed actual target and learn a reliable response on retry.
