EXCEPTION: HUMAN is an offline-capable single-player platformer with authored AI satire, not a generative AI service. No account or login is required. Campaign shows unlocked levels; progression unlocks subsequent levels. Move with the left controls, Jump and Rewrite with the right controls. Pause > Extras > Challenges opens four optional challenges. Pause > Settings & help contains support, privacy choices and ad reporting. Build 8 uses production Google AdMob IDs with non-personalized requests. Ads are limited to active gameplay and hidden in menus, loss screens and the Puzzle Box console. Consent or ad-load failures do not block gameplay. AdMob store linking and app readiness review await a public listing; ad fill may be limited or unavailable. There are no real purchases or subscriptions.
